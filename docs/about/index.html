About the researcher

CMB Research is an independent research platform maintained by Chinmay Belgaonkar.

Chinmay Belgaonkar

Independent researcher
Quantitative finance · economics · prediction markets · applied data analysis
GitHub ↗

I studied Computer Information Systems and data analytics at Georgia State University and previously worked in derivatives-clearing operations at Morgan Stanley. My research interests sit at the intersection of markets, empirical measurement, decision systems, and economics.

The common thread is methodological rather than disciplinary: take a loosely defined question, decide what can actually be measured, construct a test that could fail, and distinguish a compelling story from evidence that survives audit.

Current research interests

  • market structure and empirical asset-pricing questions;
  • prediction markets, information aggregation, and AI decision-making;
  • labour economics and Indian public microdata;
  • optimization and research methodology;
  • reproducible empirical workflows.

What this site is not

It is not a signal-selling service, a feed of cherry-picked backtests, or a claim that every research question produces alpha. Company-specific equity work will be separated from quantitative research and will disclose assumptions, uncertainty, and conflicts where relevant.

Attribution. Research on this site may be cited as work by Chinmay Belgaonkar / CMB Research. Public code and audit material are linked from major projects where available.
