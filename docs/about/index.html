Chinmay studied Computer Information Systems and data analytics at Georgia State University and previously worked in derivatives-clearing operations at Morgan Stanley. Current work spans Indian equity research, quantitative finance, prediction markets, labour economics, and applied data analysis.

The common thread is research design: turning a broad question into something measurable, building a test that can fail, and documenting what changes when the evidence does not support the original story.

Research interests

  • Indian equity research, market structure, and empirical asset-pricing questions;
  • quantitative trading research with explicit failed-test documentation;
  • prediction markets, information aggregation, and AI decision-making;
  • labour economics and Indian public microdata such as PLFS;
  • optimization, research design, and reproducible analytical workflows.

Why failed ideas stay on the site

CMB Research is not intended to be a page where every project ends with a clean positive finding. Methodological mistakes, failed holdouts, null results, and economically trivial effects remain part of the research record.

What this site is not

It is not a signal-selling service or a claim that every research question produces alpha. Company-specific equity work is kept separate from quantitative research, with assumptions, uncertainty, and relevant conflicts stated where they matter.
