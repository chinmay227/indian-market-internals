Equity Research

I want to keep fundamental company work separate from quantitative signal research. I am leaving this section empty until I have a report that is worth publishing rather than filling it with a generic stock pitch.

How I want to structure a report

A thesis should show the assumptions that are carrying it.

I do not want every report to end with a neat target price just because that is what an equity-research template usually expects. I would rather make the business drivers, valuation assumptions, risks, and falsification conditions explicit.

01

Business and industry

Revenue model, competitive structure, unit economics, key industry drivers, and what the market may already be pricing.

02

Evidence and valuation

Financial statements, operating drivers, scenario-based valuation, sensitivities, and the assumptions doing most of the work.

03

What would change my mind

Bull case, bear case, key risks, thesis-break conditions, and dated updates when the evidence changes.

Current policy: I am treating this as an equity-research section rather than a recommendation feed. Buy, sell, hold labels and target-price publication will only be added after the relevant disclosure and regulatory requirements are handled properly.
