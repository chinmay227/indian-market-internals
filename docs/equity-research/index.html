Report standard

What a company note should contain.

The objective is not to manufacture a target price for every company. Each report should make the thesis falsifiable and expose the assumptions carrying the valuation.

01

Business & industry

Revenue model, competitive structure, unit economics, key industry drivers, and what the market may already be pricing.

02

Evidence & valuation

Financial statements, operating drivers, scenario-based valuation, sensitivity, and explicit assumptions rather than one opaque target.

03

Falsification & updates

Bull case, bear case, what would change the thesis, and dated updates when the evidence changes.

Current policy: this section is framed as equity research rather than an anonymous recommendation feed. Buy/sell/hold labels and target-price publication will only be added after the relevant disclosure and regulatory requirements are handled properly.
