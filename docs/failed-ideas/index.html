Failed Ideas

Ideas that looked promising, produced null results, failed validation, or turned out to be methodologically wrong. They remain public because a research process is less credible when only the winners survive.

Failed idea #001
Look-ahead bias

Short the rebound after extreme relative weakness

A retrospective rebound peak appeared to be followed by sharp deterioration. The “entry” was selected using future information inside the next 20 sessions, manufacturing much of the pattern. The result was rejected and the test rebuilt around fixed prospective landmarks.

Failure taxonomy

What can put an idea here?

Look-ahead bias, overfitting, regime instability, failure on a locked holdout, economically trivial magnitude, data-quality problems, or a genuine null. A failed idea may still generate a better research question.
