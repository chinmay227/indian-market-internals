When does equity weakness actually persist?

An empirical study of relative weakness, episode construction, rebound dynamics, and backtest bias across a frozen Nifty 500 universe.

The replicated finding

A larger rebound inside persistent weakness is followed by modestly worse sector-relative performance.

Among stocks still weak versus both Nifty and their sector on episode Day 20, a larger already-observed rebound from the trailing-20-session trough is associated with poorer sector-relative performance over the next 40 sessions.

Discovery → validation

effect shrank ~68%

The sign replicated in 400 previously unused stocks, but the discovery estimate materially overstated the effect size.

Why Day 20 became the candidate

discovery sample only

Cluster robustness

2,000 bootstrap draws

All three predeclared clustered 95% intervals remain below zero, although the calendar-quarter upper bound is close to zero.

What this does not mean: the validated relationship is mainly sector-relative. The Nifty-relative and absolute-stock correlations are close to zero, so this is not evidence that the stock itself will necessarily fall.

The most important failed result

A strong shorting pattern disappeared when hindsight was removed.

An early experiment selected the maximum rebound inside a future 20-session window and then measured returns after that selected point. That created a mechanically favorable-looking decline.

The hindsight trap

rejected analysis

When the selected maximum occurred on Day 20, the apparent decline vanished.

Tempting result

Reject it

The result was treated as selection-induced rather than retained as alpha.

Redesign prospectively

Fixed Day 5, 10, and 20 landmarks use only information observable at the anchor.

Episode construction

Weak days were compressed into persistent states.

Consecutive sessions weak versus both Nifty and sector peers are grouped into episodes, reducing repeated counting of essentially the same state.

Methodology

Designed to make weak results disappear when they should.

01

Relative returns

Nifty 50 plus a leave-one-out equal-weight sector peer benchmark.

02

Continuous features

Magnitude, persistence, concentration, effective negative days, and evenness remain separate.

03

Episodes

Consecutive weak-both sessions form one episode.

04

Prospective anchors

Predictor history ends on Day 20; future outcomes start strictly at t+1.

05

Dependence control

Primary same-stock episode starts are spaced by more than 60 sessions.

06

Locked holdout

The 400-stock complement and success criteria were frozen before validation returns were inspected.

Known limitation: historical analysis uses current Nifty 500 membership, creating current-membership / survivorship bias.
Phase 11
