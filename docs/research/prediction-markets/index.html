What the results show

One local Qwen 3.5 9B configuration separated transparent source endpoints, preserved an explicit five-level reliability gradient, and resisted five fixed rhetoric conditions at an audited 0.50 base rate.

What they do not show

They do not establish that LLMs can infer unknown source reliability, resist manipulation generally, or identify why a price moved in a real market.

Evidence audit

All three controlled results are reproducible; the broader claim is not established.

The evidence now includes the 36-call transparent-provenance pilot, the 60-call graded reliability experiment, and the 60-call fixed-narrative stress test, with raw prompts, responses, configuration records, and regenerated analysis.

Available and verified

  • 36 transparent-pilot, 60 graded-reliability, and 60 narrative-stress decisions
  • 60/60 gradient calls succeeded with zero retries
  • All five audited levels were reported exactly in every tested context
  • All 12 trajectories were complete, monotonic, and responsive
  • All five narrative-condition medians remained 0.50, with zero median shift from neutral
  • All 12 narrative matched contexts passed the predeclared stability bounds
  • Raw prompts, responses, hashes, and inference configuration are preserved
  • Analysis regenerates from the append-only logs

Not established yet

  • No replication across prompt templates, quantizations, or model families
  • No test of whether the model can infer an unknown source reliability
  • No real-market or multi-agent evidence
  • Only five fixed rhetoric templates were tested; no adaptive adversarial narratives
  • Raw outputs from two earlier exploratory diagnostics were not recovered
Current conclusion

The transparent endpoints, explicit reliability gradient, and fixed non-evidential narrative stress test all passed. This remains evidence from controlled prompts and one model configuration—not proof of real-world manipulation resistance.

Interpretation boundary

This is a clean competence result: the model preserved a reliability parameter that the prompt explicitly supplied. It does not show that the model can discover source quality from ambiguous evidence. The fixed-narrative stress test below asks whether irrelevant persuasive language disrupts that result.
