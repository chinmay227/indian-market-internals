What the results show

What they do not show

Evidence audit

Both controlled results are reproducible; the broader claim is not established.

The evidence now includes the 36-call transparent-provenance pilot and the completed 60-call graded reliability experiment, with raw prompts, responses, configuration records, and regenerated analysis.

Available and verified

  • 36 transparent-pilot decisions and 60 graded-reliability decisions
  • 60/60 gradient calls succeeded with zero retries
  • All five audited levels were reported exactly in every tested context
  • All 12 trajectories were complete, monotonic, and responsive
  • Raw prompts, responses, hashes, and inference configuration are preserved
  • Analysis regenerates from the append-only logs

Not established yet

  • No replication across prompt templates, quantizations, or model families
  • No test of whether the model can infer an unknown source reliability
  • No real-market or multi-agent evidence
  • No test yet with persuasive narratives at fixed audited reliability
  • Raw outputs from two earlier exploratory diagnostics were not recovered
Current conclusion

The transparent endpoints and the explicit reliability gradient both passed. This supports moving to a harder stress test, but it remains evidence of controlled instruction-following and calibration—not proof of real-world manipulation resistance.

Interpretation boundary

This is a clean competence result: the model preserved a reliability parameter that the prompt explicitly supplied. It does not show that the model can discover source quality from ambiguous evidence. The next stress test should hold audited reliability fixed while adding non-evidential persuasive or manipulative narratives.
