Research standards

Evidence before conviction.

The goal is not to produce a constant stream of positive findings. The goal is to make it difficult for weak findings to survive.

01

Separate discovery from validation

Exploratory choices are labeled as exploratory. Confirmatory specifications are frozen before the relevant holdout is inspected when practical.

02

Preserve negative results

Nulls, failed hypotheses, and methodological errors stay in the archive rather than disappearing from the narrative.

03

Report shrinkage

A weaker validation result is not hidden. Effect-size deterioration is evidence about how optimistic discovery was.

Failed idea #001

The shorting result that looked excellent — until the anchor was audited.

An early weakness experiment chose the maximum rebound using future information and then measured what happened after it. The apparent deterioration was largely mechanical.

Rejected analysis

Short the rebound after extreme weakness

The result was removed from the candidate set rather than re-labeled as alpha. A fixed Day-20 redesign produced a much smaller, sector-relative relationship.

  1. Tempting pattern: retrospective rebound peaks appeared to precede sharp declines.
  2. Audit: the rebound peak was selected from the next 20 sessions, so the anchor contained future information.
  3. Smoking gun: the apparent decline disappeared when the selected maximum occurred at the end of the search window.
  4. Redesign: precommitted Day 5, 10, and 20 landmarks used only information known at the anchor.
